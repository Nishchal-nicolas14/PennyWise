
# PennyWise 

A personal expense tracker designed to help users understand their spending habits, manage daily expenses, and take better control of their finances.

## 📌 Problem Statement

Managing personal finances can become difficult when expenses are spread across different categories and recorded inconsistently. Many people struggle to track where their money goes, identify unnecessary spending, and understand how much they can save.

Without a clear overview of income, expenses, and spending patterns, it becomes challenging to maintain a budget and make informed financial decisions.

## 💡 Our Solution

PennyWise aims to provide a centralized platform where users can manage their personal finances through a clean, intuitive, and modern dashboard.

The application will allow users to record and categorize transactions, monitor their income and expenses, visualize spending patterns, and track their financial activity in one place.

By presenting financial information through meaningful summaries and visual insights, PennyWise aims to make expense tracking simpler, more organized, and accessible.

## 🎯 Project Goals

- Simplify daily expense tracking.
- Help users understand their spending habits.
- Provide a clear overview of income and expenses.
- Make budget management easier.
- Deliver a clean, responsive, and user-friendly experience.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

*Planned technologies*

- Frontend: React.js
- Styling: CSS / Tailwind CSS
- Backend: Node.js and Express.js
- Database: MongoDB

## 🚧 Project Status

Currently in development. Features and implementation details may evolve as the project progresses.

## 🎯 Vision

To make personal finance management easier by turning everyday financial data into clear, useful insights that help users spend smarter and save better.
